Summary

Researcher in computational astronomy specializing in planet formation simulations, studying the formation of planets from planetesimals with large-scale, self-consistent N-body simulations. PI of three consecutive "Fugaku" Young Researcher projects and a Miyabi "Large-scale HPC Challenge" project; chief developer of GPLUM-GPU, a high-performance GPU-accelerated N-body simulation code for planet formation.

Research Fields: Natural Sciences / Space and Planetary Science / Planetary Formation; Information and Communication / High-Performance Computing / Parallel Computing
